# FinAuditPro — Risk Assessment & Materiality Engine

## 1. Risk Evaluation Model (SA 315 / SA 330)
Audit risk is modeled mathematically as:
$$\text{Audit Risk (AR)} = \text{Inherent Risk (IR)} \times \text{Control Risk (CR)} \times \text{Detection Risk (DR)}$$

Assertions evaluated for each account head:
- **Completeness (C)**
- **Existence / Occurrence (E)**
- **Accuracy / Valuation (A/V)**
- **Rights & Obligations (R)**
- **Presentation & Disclosure (P)**
- **Cut-off (CO)**

## 2. Materiality Determination Engine (SA 320)
- **Overall Materiality (OM):** Calculated from Revenue ($0.5\% - 2\%$), PBT ($5\% - 10\%$), Total Assets ($0.5\% - 1\%$), or Net Worth ($1\% - 5\%$).
- **Performance Materiality (PM):** $50\% - 75\%$ of OM.
- **Clearly Trivial Threshold (CTT):** $1\% - 5\%$ of OM.
