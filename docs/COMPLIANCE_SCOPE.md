# FinAuditPro Compliance Scope & Regulatory Disclaimer (v1.0.0)

## 1. Compliance Disclaimer & Professional Responsibility
FinAuditPro is an offline-first desktop software application designed to provide mathematical calculations, structured checklists, document organization, and audit working paper management for Indian Chartered Accountants (CAs).

> **Statutory Notice**:
> FinAuditPro provides software workflows and automated controls intended to support audit procedures performed under applicable professional standards (such as ICAI Standards on Auditing, CARO 2020, and the Companies Act, 2013).
> 
> The software does NOT:
> 1. Grant official ICAI accreditation or automatic regulatory compliance.
> 2. Issue or certify an independent auditor's report.
> 3. Replace professional judgment, skepticism, or evidence evaluation.
> 
> The Engagement Partner and the audit firm remain exclusively responsible for the design, execution, documentation, sufficiency of evidence, and formal opinions rendered on any audit engagement.

---

## 2. Privacy & Data Protection (DPDP Act 2023)
- **Zero Outbound Client Data Transmission**: FinAuditPro does not communicate with external cloud servers, third-party analytics, or telemetry platforms.
- **Local Data Storage**: All client financial datasets, confidential trial balances, working papers, and evidence documents are stored exclusively on the local workstation or firm-controlled storage.
- **Encryption at Rest**: Column-level encryption and password-protected encrypted backups ensure confidential client data is protected in accordance with professional ethical standards.
