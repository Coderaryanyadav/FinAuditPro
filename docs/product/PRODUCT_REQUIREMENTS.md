# FinAuditPro — Product Vision & Requirements

## 1. Product Vision
**FinAuditPro** is an offline-first desktop operating system engineered for Indian Chartered Accountants (CAs), audit managers, article assistants, and engagement partners. It transforms statutory auditing under the Companies Act 2013 and ICAI Standards on Auditing (SAs) into a deterministic, cryptographically verified digital workflow.

## 2. Key Functional Modules
1. **Engagement Hierarchy:** Rigid 3-tier isolation (`Firm -> Client -> Engagement`).
2. **Financial Ingestion:** Automated column header matching and integer-paise ingestion for Trial Balances, General Ledgers, and Fixed Asset Registers.
3. **Statutory Lead Schedules:** Auto-mapping to Schedule III Division I & II formats.
4. **Audit Sampling Engine:** Deterministic Monetary-Unit Sampling (MUS) and stratified sampling (SA 530).
5. **Materiality Determination:** SA 320 compliant Overall Materiality (OM), Performance Materiality (PM), and Clearly Trivial Threshold (CTT) calculators.
6. **Statutory Compliance Engines:** Dedicated checkers for CARO 2020 (21 clauses) and Tax Audit Form 3CD (44 clauses).
7. **Report Assembly:** Automated generator for Independent Auditor's Reports (SA 700 / 705 / 706) with UDIN watermarks and sealed PDF exports.
